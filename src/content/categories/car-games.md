## Types of car games

Car games are easy to group by how you play them. Some focus on speed and racing against the clock, some on driving skill, and some on precise parking or stunts. Knowing which type you enjoy helps you pick a game that suits your taste. The two most common types on Playzoni are described below.

### Driving games

Driving games focus on everyday road driving. You steer through traffic, handle corners and keep your speed under control. They suit players who like to explore a route rather than race against others. Browse the full list of [driving games](/driving-games).

### Parking games

Parking games test your precision in tight spaces. Each level sets a small target, and you line up the car without touching the edges. Take it slow at first, because the best scores come from patience rather than speed. Browse the full list of [parking games](/parking-games).

## Playing car games on mobile

Many car games use on-screen controls on phones. Tap or hold the buttons to steer, accelerate and brake. Turning your phone sideways gives you more room to see the track. Each game page lists the controls for mobile and for desktop, so you know what to expect before you start.

If a game feels hard to control at first, try a few practice laps and watch how the car reacts to each button. Short sessions are often easier than long ones, and you can always come back to a game later when you feel ready.
