## Types of 2 player games

These games fall into a few families. Racing games put two players on the same track, so the clear winner is whoever crosses the line first. Fighting and battle games give each player a character or a force to command. Turn-based games let you take a move each, which suits quieter, thoughtful play. Word and quiz games reward quick thinking and a good memory.

### Racing and fighting games

Racing and fighting games are fast and direct. You see your opponent all the time, so the match feels personal. Learn the basic controls first, then watch how the other player moves. In racing games, a clean line through a corner often beats a risky shortcut. In fighting games, staying calm under pressure is the real skill.

### Turn-based and word games

Turn-based and word games give each player time to think. You make one move, then the other player responds. They suit players who like to plan and who enjoy a calmer pace. Word games reward a wide vocabulary and quick recall, so take turns that feel fair and do not rush your answers.

## Playing 2 player games on mobile

Some two player games on Playzoni use touch areas on each side of a phone screen. Hold the phone sideways so each player has room for their thumbs. Games built for a computer usually need a keyboard, so check the game page for the controls before you start. Each page lists the controls for mobile and desktop.

If you play on one phone with a friend, pass it carefully between turns and tell each other which side is yours. A short practice round at the start helps both players find their controls and avoid mix-ups in the first important moments of the match.
