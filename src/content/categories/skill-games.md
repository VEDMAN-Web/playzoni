## Types of skill games

Skill games group by the skill they test. Timing games ask you to press or release at exactly the right moment. Aiming games ask you to judge distance and angle. Balance and stacking games test steady hands. Slicing and flicking games reward quick, clean movement. Knowing which skill you enjoy helps you find games that feel satisfying.

### Timing games

Timing games depend on a moment. You watch a bar, a marker or a moving target, and you act when it lines up. Start by watching a few rounds without rushing, so you learn the pattern before you try to win. Small delays are the most common reason for missed shots, so aim to act a split second early rather than late.

### Aiming and stacking games

Aiming and stacking games ask you to place things with care. You judge the angle or the drop point, then watch the result. Take your time with each placement, because one mistake can bring the whole tower down. Slow, deliberate moves usually earn better scores than fast, careless ones.

## Playing skill games on mobile

Most skill games on Playzoni use a tap, a hold or a swipe, which suits a phone well. Keep your thumb steady and avoid pressing too hard, since that can make your timing drift. Turn your phone sideways for games that need a wider view. Each game page explains the controls for mobile and desktop.

If you keep missing the same moment, slow down for a round and count the beats in your head. Many players find that a steady rhythm works better than reacting as fast as possible. Short sessions are often more useful than long ones, because you can notice your mistakes while they are still fresh.
