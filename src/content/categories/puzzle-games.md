## Puzzle Games on Playzoni

Puzzle games reward careful thinking. On Playzoni you can match tiles, sort colours, fill grids and work through logic challenges, all in free browser games that open on the page when you press Play. There is nothing to install, so a short puzzle fits easily into a coffee break.

Puzzles come in many forms. Some ask you to spot a pattern and place numbers or shapes correctly. Others ask you to rearrange pieces until everything lines up. A few add a small time limit or a score, which turns a calm puzzle into a quick challenge. Begin with a puzzle that explains its rules on the first screen, then try a different type to keep your thinking fresh.

- Jewel Coloring: fill the picture with colour one piece at a time.
- Jewel Magic: match jewels in a bright board that rewards clever combinations.
- Sudoku: fill every row, column and box with the numbers from 1 to 9.

## Types of puzzle games

Puzzle games are easy to group by the thinking they need. Number puzzles such as Sudoku ask for logic and patience. Matching games depend on spotting shapes and colours quickly. Sorting and sliding games test how well you plan several moves ahead. Picking the type you enjoy helps you find games that feel rewarding rather than frustrating.

### Matching and sorting games

Matching and sorting games are the most common on the site. You combine tiles, pour colours into the right containers or group items by type. They are relaxed to play because each move makes the board a little tidier. Look for patterns in the first few moves, and do not rush the last tiles, since they are often the hardest.

### Number and logic games

Number and logic games ask you to fill a grid with rules in mind. Sudoku is the classic example. Work out the easy squares first, write down candidates when you need to, and check each row before you move on. These games are ideal when you want to slow down and reason it out.

## Playing puzzle games on mobile

Most puzzles on Playzoni work with taps and drags, which suit touch screens well. Use your thumb to drag pieces and tap to select, and turn your phone sideways when a board is wide. Each game page lists the controls for mobile and desktop, so the first few moves will feel familiar.

If a puzzle feels stuck, step away for a minute and come back with fresh eyes. Many players find that a short break solves the problem more quickly than hours of trial and error. You can always return to an unfinished puzzle later.
