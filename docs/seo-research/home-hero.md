# Home hero list — easy games first (R29)

Made by the planner on 11 Nov 2026 at the owner's request: simple one-touch games lead the home page, complex games come after.

Rules used:
- One-touch or very simple controls, short rounds, fun in the first 10 seconds.
- Works on phones (made for mobile; the play test confirms).
- Good, readable thumbnail at small size.
- No famous brand names. **Subway Surfers** and **Count Masters** are owned by other companies; the feeds only have unofficial copies, so similar original runners and crowd games are used instead.
